Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

This policy describes how Tameware (“we”) handles information when you use Tameware Outliner (the “App”), including the copy hosted on tameware.com and related demos.

Summary

  • You can use the App locally without signing in. Outline content then stays in your browser or in OPML files you export.
  • If you choose Sign in with Google, the App stores outlines in your Google Drive (in a folder named “Tameware Outliner”), not on Tameware servers.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

Who we are

Contact: adam@tameware.com (or info@tameware.com).

Information we process

Local use (no Google sign-in)

Outline text you type is kept in the browser’s page memory until you export it or close the tab. Import/Export uses files you choose on your device. We do not receive that content.

Google Sign-In and Google Drive (optional)

If you sign in with Google, the App requests access limited to:

  • Basic Google account profile information (such as name and email) so we can show who is signed in.
  • Google Drive access with the drive.file scope: files and folders the App creates or that you open with the App—typically the “Tameware Outliner” folder and .opml outlines inside it.

Outline files live in your Google account. Sharing (read-only or editable links) uses Google Drive’s sharing settings so people you share with can open those files according to the permissions you grant.

Google’s handling of account and Drive data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Technical and hosting data

Hosting providers (for example when you load the App from our website or GitHub Pages) may process standard server logs such as IP address, user agent, and request time. We do not use that to build marketing profiles of Outliner users.

How we use information

  • To provide the Outliner features you request (edit, save, open, share).
  • To show your signed-in identity in the App UI.
  • To respond if you contact us.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Outline content you save to Drive is shared only when you create share links or otherwise share files through Google Drive.

Retention

Drive files remain until you delete them (or empty Trash) in Google Drive. You can revoke the App’s access anytime in your Google Account permissions. Local browser content lasts only for the session unless you export a file.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy: adam@tameware.com.
